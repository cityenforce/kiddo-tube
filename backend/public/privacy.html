1. Introduction & Our Mission

Welcome to KiddoTube (referred to as "the App", "we", "our", or "us"). KiddoTube is operated by Sheraz Ali Developer ("Service Provider") to provide families and children with a curated, safe, and positive video streaming experience.

KiddoTube operates strictly under a Parent-Managed Walled Garden model. Unlike open video-sharing platforms, KiddoTube contains no open internet browser, no algorithmic recommendations, and no direct communication features. Children can only explore content that their parent or legal guardian has intentionally added and approved for them.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when parents and children use the KiddoTube application on Android mobile and tablet devices, in full compliance with the Google Play Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K).

2. Information Collection & Use

A. Information Collected from Children (Under 13 / 16)

KiddoTube DOES NOT collect personally identifiable information (PII) from children.

  • No Real Names or Contact Data: We never ask children for their full legal name, email address, home address, phone number, school, or social media handles.
  • Pseudonymous Profiles Only: Child explorer profiles are created solely by the parent and consist only of a nickname (e.g. "Fatima" or "Explorer"), an avatar emoji (e.g. 🚀, 🐧, 🦁), and an age band (e.g. 3–5, 6–8) to help parents tailor content.
  • No Biometric or Visual Data: We do not access the device camera, microphone, or photo gallery for child users.
  • No Precise Location Tracking: We never access or track the device's GPS or precise geographic location.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect Google Advertising IDs (GAID) or persistent device identifiers for advertising or user tracking.

B. Information Collected from Parents & Legal Guardians (Adults 18+)

To set up and administer parental controls, the parent creates an account providing:

  • Parent Email Address: Used strictly for account sign-in, essential security notices, and one-time password (OTP) verification for password recovery.
  • Parent Password & PIN: Cryptographically hashed using industry-standard bcrypt. Plaintext passwords and PINs are never stored or visible to anyone.
  • Curated Whitelist Data: YouTube video IDs, playlist IDs, and channel IDs that the parent chooses to whitelist for their children.
  • Daily Screen Time Limits: Time limits configured by parents to supervise their children's viewing habits.

💡 Note on Child Login: Child accounts use a local username and child password created by the parent inside the Parent Dashboard. These credentials belong to the family account and are never shared with external advertisers or third parties.

3. Content Moderation & Safe Streaming Architecture

KiddoTube guarantees a safe digital space through its unique architecture:

  • Zero Open Browsing: Children cannot navigate to external websites, tap outbound web links, or browse unapproved channels.
  • Zero Social Interactions: The application contains NO public comments, NO live chat, NO direct messaging, and NO social media sharing buttons. Children can never be contacted by strangers.
  • Whitelisted In-App Search: The search feature inside the child mode searches only within the parent's approved video database. Children cannot search YouTube or the open internet directly.
  • Hidden Adult Gate: All parental settings, account details, and video approvals are locked behind a parental authentication gate (PIN / password) to ensure children cannot modify safety settings.

4. Third-Party Video Services (YouTube API Services)

KiddoTube enables parents to import and stream educational and entertaining video content hosted on YouTube using official YouTube API Services and embedded web player components.

In accordance with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Google Developer Policies:

  • By using KiddoTube to play YouTube videos, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Video streaming and player infrastructure are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Restricted Kid-Safe Playback: KiddoTube embeds videos using strict safety parameters (modestbranding=1, rel=0, iv_load_policy=3, controls=0, disablekb=1, and youtube-nocookie.com origin) to suppress related unapproved video suggestions and native annotations.
  • KiddoTube does NOT transmit any child personal identifiers, viewing histories, or account profiles to YouTube or Google.
  • KiddoTube does not store or distribute raw video files; streaming occurs directly through official embedded player components in accordance with YouTube guidelines.

5. Zero Commercial Ads & No Behavioral Tracking

KiddoTube is proudly committed to protecting young minds:

  • NO Ad SDKs: We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs (e.g. AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network).
  • NO Targeted or Behavioral Advertising: We never track children's behavior across apps or websites to serve targeted ads.
  • NO Cookies or Tracking Pixels: KiddoTube mobile app does not use marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-app analytics for profiling.
  • NO Data Selling: We never sell, lease, rent, or trade user data (parent or child) to data brokers, advertisers, or any commercial entities.

6. Screen Time & Digital Well-Being

KiddoTube promotes balanced digital habits in accordance with pediatric health recommendations:

  • Customizable Daily Timers: Parents can configure daily screen time limits (from 15 minutes up to 8 hours) globally or individually per child.
  • Automated Lock Screen: Once the daily allowance is reached, video playback immediately stops and the screen locks with a friendly "Time Out" screen until parent PIN authorization.
  • 20-Minute Eye Break Reminders: Parents can enable visual break reminders every 20 minutes to prevent eye fatigue.

7. Parental Rights, Data Retention & Complete Deletion

Parents and legal guardians retain complete authority over all data stored in KiddoTube:

  • Reviewing & Editing Data: Parents can view and update child nicknames, passwords, and approved video libraries at any time via the Parent Dashboard.
  • Child Profile Deletion: Parents can instantly delete any child profile. Deletion immediately purges the profile, encrypted credentials, and associated watch time records from our database.
  • Full Account Deletion: Parents can delete their entire account and all family records directly in the app or by submitting a deletion request. When deleted, all records are permanently erased within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Data Retention Policy: We retain parent account data only as long as the parent maintains an active account. If an account is inactive or requested to be removed, it is completely deleted.

🗑️ How to Request Data Deletion: You can delete any child profile or your parent account directly inside the app under Parent Dashboard → Settings, or by emailing us at cityenforce@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

8. Information Security & Technical Safeguards

We take the protection of family data seriously and employ robust technical safeguards:

  • End-to-End Transport Security: All network communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern HTTPS and Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+).
  • Cryptographic Hashing: Passwords and Parental PINs are one-way hashed with bcrypt using strong salt factors.
  • Database Isolation: Family databases enforce multi-tenant isolation so no parent or child can ever access another family's settings or profiles.

9. International Compliance (COPPA, GDPR-K & California)

  • COPPA (United States): Compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. Verifiable parental consent is obtained prior to account activation. No child personal data is collected or disclosed.
  • GDPR / GDPR-K (European Union & UK): Parents have the right to access, rectify, port, and erase their personal data under Articles 15–20 of GDPR. Processing is grounded in parental consent and contractual necessity to provide the service.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information of any users, including minors under 16 years of age.
